Praying a novena

Tap Choose a novena, pick one, add your intention if you like, and tap Start today. Each day the app shows that day's prayer. Read it slowly, then tap Amen to mark the day prayed. After nine days the novena moves to Finished.

If you miss a day

You can start again from day 1. With Novena Tracker Pro you can catch up instead, carrying on from the day you missed.

Reminders

A daily reminder comes at the time you choose in Settings, only on days a novena still has a prayer to say.

Novena Tracker Pro

Pro adds Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, the full library of 30 novenas, several novenas at once, catching up on a missed day, an intentions journal and feast day suggestions so you finish on the feast. It is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year, with a free week on the yearly plan. Restore or manage it in Settings.

The prayers

Every novena prayer is original wording written for this app. The Our Father, Hail Mary and Glory Be are traditional prayers.
